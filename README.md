## Hi there 👋

# Short Bio

Lin Zhao is an Assistant Professor in the [Department of Biomedical Engineering](https://biomedical.njit.edu/), [New Jersey Institute of Technology (NJIT)](https://www.njit.edu/), and the Director of the Machine Intelligence in Medicine and Imaging Lab (MI<sup>2</sup> Lab). He was a Senior Research Scientist at United Imaging Intelligence (UII) America from 2023 to 2025. He received his PhD degree in Computer Science from the University of Georgia in 2023 under the supervision of [Prof. Tianming Liu](https://cobweb.cs.uga.edu/~tliu/). He received his bachelor's degree from Northwestern Polytechnical University in 2017. His current research interests include brain-inspired AI, large foundation models, vision-language modeling, and their applications in medical imaging. He has published over 50 papers <a href='https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=RxG1Wj8AAAAJ'><img src="https://img.shields.io/endpoint?logo=Google%20Scholar&url=https%3A%2F%2Fcdn.jsdelivr.net%2Fgh%2Flin-zhao-research%2Flin-zhao-research.github.io@google-scholar-stats%2Fgs_data_shieldsio.json&labelColor=f6f6f6&color=9cf&style=flat&label=citations"></a> in leading AI and medical imaging journals and conference proceedings.


# Research Interests

- **Brain-Inspired AI**: Uncovering computational principles of biological intelligence to advance reasoning, learning, and generalization in AI.
- **Medical Foundation Models and Agents**: Advancing general-purpose medical intelligence through multimodal foundation models, world models, and AI agents.
- **Computational Neuroscience**: Integrating multimodal data and representation learning to uncover the principles of brain structure and function and elucidate mechanisms of neurobiological disease.
